# ServiceCNL — Company SPOC Instructions

## Purpose

This document provides detailed instructions for using the **ServiceCNL onboarding system** as a Company SPOC.

The ServiceCNL onboarding process is used to obtain **security screening and training for new contractors who require access to a CNL site**.

> **Important:** Renewals, upgrades and downgrades do **not** go through this onboarding process.

---

## 1. What Is Required to Get Started?

Each company must have:

- a company profile created in the ServiceCNL system; and
- a designated person responsible for submitting requests.

To get started, contact:

**contractormanagement@cnl.ca**

---

## 2. What Is the ServiceCNL Onboarding Process For?

The ServiceCNL onboarding request is used for **new contractors requiring security screening and training to access a CNL site**.

This process is not used for:

- renewals;
- security/access upgrades;
- security/access downgrades.

---

## 3. What Is a Company SPOC?

The **Company SPOC** is the person designated by the company to submit contractor requests through ServiceCNL.

The Company SPOC receives a **username and password** allowing them to log into the system.

---

## 4. How Does the Company SPOC Access ServiceCNL?

The Company SPOC accesses the ServiceCNL system through the designated **ServiceNow login**.

Refer to the official source document or approved ServiceCNL resource for the current login link.

---

## 5. What Information Is Required to Begin a Request?

A separate request must be submitted for **each worker**.

The Company SPOC will need:

- the worker's **full legal name**; and
- the worker's **personal email address**.

The personal email address is used to communicate with the CNL Security Personnel team and to send system updates to the worker.

---

## 6. Who Is the CNL Contractor Representative?

The **CNL Contractor Representative** is the CNL employee responsible for the contractors while they are working at a CNL site.

This person is normally someone associated with the project or a construction superintendent.

The Company SPOC must know who the CNL Contractor Representative is **before submitting the onboarding request**.

---

## 7. What Information Is Needed From the CNL Contractor Representative?

Certain information is required when submitting an onboarding request.

The Company SPOC must know:

- whether the worker is required to be a **Nuclear Energy Worker**;
- whether **bioassay** is required for the worker;
- what **security level** is required.

> For the Cummins-specific values used when onboarding technicians, refer to the **CNL Access and Compliance** procedure.

---

## 8. What Happens After the Onboarding Request Is Submitted?

After submission, the **CNL Contractor Representative** must approve or reject the request.

The Company SPOC should ensure the Contractor Representative knows that a request has been submitted so that it can be reviewed promptly.

Once the Contractor Representative approves the request, it is sent to the **CNL Personnel Security team** to begin the security screening process.

---

## 9. What Is the Worker Required to Do?

The worker receives **two emails** from the ServiceCNL system:

- one containing their username;
- one containing their password.

The worker must use these credentials to log into the system and begin completing their assigned tasks.

> **Important:** Tasks should be completed as soon as possible. The request may be cancelled if there is no activity.

---

## 10. Does the Company SPOC Receive Notifications?

Yes.

The Company SPOC can monitor updates for each onboarding request through the **ServiceCNL dashboard**.

The Company SPOC will also receive **email updates throughout the process**.

---

## 11. What Other Tasks Must the Worker Complete?

The worker is required to complete orientation training through **ISNetworld (ISN)**.

The required orientation includes:

- **Contractor Site Orientation**
- **Supplier Code of Conduct**
- **Group 4 Radiation Protection Orientation**

---

## 12. Where Are the Dosimetry Forms Located?

Dosimetry forms are available in the **Contractor Knowledge Article** located on the main ServiceCNL dashboard after logging into the system.

---

## 13. What Other Tasks Must the Company SPOC Complete?

### Enter the Start Date

The Company SPOC will receive a task requiring them to enter a **Start Date** into ServiceCNL.

This task is available through the Company SPOC dashboard.

The Company SPOC should work with the **CNL Contractor Representative** to determine an appropriate start date.

### Schedule the Security Briefing

The Company SPOC must contact CNL Personnel Security to schedule the worker's security briefing.

Contact:

**personnelsecurityscreeningcontractors@cnl.ca**

The **CNL Contractor Representative must be CC'd** on this email.

The security briefing may be conducted:

- in person; or
- remotely.

The process includes the **final security briefing** and issuance of a **security access badge**, if required.
