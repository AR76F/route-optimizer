# Canadian Nuclear Laboratories (CNL) — Access and Compliance

## Purpose

This document explains the requirements and procedures related to technician access to **Canadian Nuclear Laboratories (CNL)**.

It covers three areas:

- requesting CNL access for a new technician;
- maintaining existing security access and annual CNL training;
- verifying technician compliance through Cummins compliance resources.

---

# 1. New Access

New technician access to CNL is requested through the **ServiceCNL onboarding system**.

This onboarding process is specifically for **new contractors requiring security screening and training**.

> **Important:** Renewals, upgrades and downgrades do not go through the new contractor onboarding process.

## Information Required for a Cummins Technician

A separate onboarding request must be submitted for each technician.

When completing the request, use the following CNL-specific information:

- **CNL Contractor Representative:** Adam Corrigan
- **Nuclear Energy Worker Status Required:** YES
- **Bioassay Requirements:** NO
- **Security Screening Level:** Site Access Security Clearance
- **Class Category:** Class 1 – Onsite worker needing regular access to Protected Area
- **Required Training:** CNL Safety Orientation AND Group 4 Radiation Protection Training
- **Training Location:** Online – ISN

The technician's **full legal name** and **personal email address** are also required for the request.

The personal email address is used by CNL Security Personnel and the ServiceCNL system to communicate with the technician.

## Company SPOC

The **Company SPOC** is the designated person responsible for submitting onboarding requests on behalf of Cummins.

The Company SPOC receives credentials to access the ServiceCNL system and can monitor submitted requests through the ServiceCNL dashboard.

If a company profile or Company SPOC access needs to be established, contact:

**contractormanagement@cnl.ca**

## After Submission

After the onboarding request is submitted, the **CNL Contractor Representative** must approve or reject the request.

The Contractor Representative should be made aware that a request has been submitted so it can be approved promptly.

Once approved, the request is sent to **CNL Personnel Security** to begin the security screening process.

The technician will receive two emails from the system:

- one containing their username;
- one containing their password.

The technician must use these credentials to access the system and complete their assigned tasks.

> **Important:** Assigned tasks should be completed promptly. The onboarding request may be cancelled if there is no activity.

The Company SPOC can monitor updates for each request through the **ServiceCNL dashboard** and will also receive email updates throughout the process.

## Training

The technician must complete the required orientation and radiation protection training through **ISNetworld (ISN)**.

CNL onboarding documentation identifies the required orientation training as including:

- Contractor Site Orientation;
- Supplier Code of Conduct;
- Group 4 Radiation Protection Orientation.

## Start Date and Final Security Briefing

The Company SPOC will receive a task in ServiceCNL to enter the technician's **Start Date**.

The start date should be coordinated with the CNL Contractor Representative.

The Company SPOC must also contact CNL Personnel Security to schedule the technician's final security briefing:

**personnelsecurityscreeningcontractors@cnl.ca**

The **CNL Contractor Representative should be CC'd** on this email.

The final security briefing may take place **in person or remotely** and includes issuance of a security access badge when required.

# 2. Access and Training Renewal

CNL **security clearance** and **CNL-required training** have different validity periods and must be treated separately.

## Security Clearance

Once a technician has obtained their CNL security clearance, the security clearance is valid for **5 years**.

Security clearance therefore does **not** require annual renewal.

## Annual CNL Training

CNL-required training must be completed **once per year**.

CNL normally sends reminders in advance when technician training is approaching its expiry date.

The following requirements should be monitored individually:

- **G4 — Group 4 Radiation Protection Training**
- **CSO — CNL Safety Orientation**

G4 and CSO may have **different expiry dates for the same technician**. Both should therefore be monitored independently.

## Checking Training Renewal

Technicians can verify their training renewal and expiry information by logging into their own **ISNetworld (ISN) account**.

The required annual training takes approximately **4 hours to complete**.

## Billing for Annual Training

Time spent completing the required annual CNL training is **not billable separately to CNL**.

The cost associated with the required training is already included in the hourly rates paid by CNL.

## Renewal Process — Detail to Be Confirmed

It has not yet been confirmed whether the required courses automatically become available for renewal in the technician's ISN account or whether they must first be reassigned or initiated by CNL or another responsible party.

Until this process is confirmed, technicians should use their ISN account to verify their training status and renewal information.

> **Do not assume a course reassignment or renewal workflow until this process has been confirmed.**

# 3. Cummins Compliance Tracking

Cummins maintains an internal compliance resource that can be used to check technician compliance information associated with ISN/CNL requirements.

The resource provides technician-level information including:

- Employee name
- ISN ID
- Compliance status
- Additional compliance details

Compliance may be displayed using status indicators such as **Green** or **Red**.

## Compliance Questions

For questions regarding a technician's compliance status or compliance requirements, contact the **Cummins Compliance Team**:

**CSSNA.CM.compliance@cummins.com**

When contacting the Compliance Team, provide the **name of the technician(s)** in question so the team can review the individual records and provide additional details.

> **Important:** Do not assume the cause of a Red or other non-compliant status based only on the status indicator. Review the technician's compliance details or contact the Compliance Team for clarification.

---

# Important Distinctions

- **New technician:** Use the ServiceCNL onboarding process.
- **Security clearance:** Valid for **5 years**.
- **CNL training:** Renewed **annually**.
- **G4 and CSO:** Track their expiry dates separately.
- **Training status/expiry:** Technician can verify through their **ISN account**.
- **Compliance issue or uncertainty:** Contact the **Cummins Compliance Team**.
- **Do not submit a new ServiceCNL onboarding request simply to renew existing access or training.**
