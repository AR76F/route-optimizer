# Bank of Canada — Security Access

## Purpose

This procedure explains the security screening process that must be completed for an employee or technician who requires access to a **Bank of Canada (BoC) site**.

Bank of Canada security screening is separate from security clearances obtained for other government buildings. An employee who is already cleared to enter another government facility **cannot use that clearance as authorization to access a Bank of Canada site**.

## Security Screening Process

### 1. Personal Information Request (PIR)

A **Personal Information Request (PIR)** form will be sent to the employer or employee requiring access.

The individual requiring access must:

1. Complete the PIR form.
2. Send the completed form to:
   **facilities-RFA@bank-banque-canada.ca**
3. Include:
   - the Bank of Canada site location for which access is required;
   - the expected frequency of access.

---

### 2. Mintz Screening Invitation

After the PIR has been submitted, allow **up to 48 hours** to receive the Mintz screening invitation.

The Mintz invitation may be delivered to the employee's **spam or junk folder**, so these folders should be checked if the invitation does not appear in the main inbox.

If no Mintz invitation has been received within 48 hours, including in spam/junk folders, contact:

**facilities-RFA@bank-banque-canada.ca**

for assistance.

### Important — Mintz Expiration

The Mintz invitation must be completed **within 10 days of receiving it**.

If it is not completed within 10 days, the invitation will be cancelled and a **new Mintz submission will be required**.

---

### 3. Fingerprints — If Requested

After passing the Mintz screening, the employee may be asked to provide fingerprints to assist with identification.

If fingerprints are required:

1. The employee will receive an email containing a **Commissionaires fingerprint request form**.
2. Bring the request form to a **Commissionaires office** in the employee's area.
3. Complete the fingerprint process.
4. The Commissionaires will provide a document after the fingerprints have been taken.
5. Send this document to:

**facilities-RFA@bank-banque-canada.ca**

Fingerprints are therefore **not automatically required for every applicant**. They are completed when requested as part of the screening process.

---

### 4. Phone Interview — If Requested

Depending on the findings of the Mintz screening, the employee may also be asked to complete a **phone interview**.

The interview normally lasts approximately **5–10 minutes**, depending on the situation.

---

### 5. Declaration Kit

After all required Mintz processes have been completed, the employee will receive a **Declaration Kit**.

The employee must:

1. Sign all fields highlighted in yellow.
2. Obtain the required witness authorization signature.

The witness:

- cannot be the employee;
- may be a coworker, family member, or friend;
- must be **over 18 years old**.

---

### 6. Employee Headshot

A headshot of the employee must be submitted with the signed Declaration Kit.

The headshot must have a **white background**.

Send the headshot as an attachment in the **same email as the signed Declaration Kit**.

---

### 7. Security Profile and Site Access

Once all required screening steps have been completed, allow **up to 48 hours** for:

- the employee's Bank of Canada security profile to be completed; and
- the requested site access to be linked to the employee's BOC security profile.

Once this process is complete, the employee may access the identified Bank of Canada site.

---

## Security Access Renewal

The Bank of Canada monitors the expiry dates of technician security access.

Approximately **2–3 months before an access expiry**, the **Bank of Canada Screening Department** typically provides a list of upcoming expiries to the **Bank of Canada Facilities Department**.

The Facilities Department then follows up with **Cummins** regarding technicians whose security access is approaching expiry.

### Renewal Follow-Up

When Cummins is contacted by the Bank of Canada Facilities Department:

1. Identify the technician(s) whose security access is approaching expiry.
2. Follow the renewal instructions provided by the Bank of Canada.
3. Provide any information or documentation requested for the renewal.
4. Follow up as required until the technician's access renewal is completed.

---

## Important Notes

- A security clearance for another government building **does not provide access to Bank of Canada sites**.
- Always identify the **specific Bank of Canada site** and **frequency of access** when submitting the PIR.
- Check spam and junk folders for the Mintz invitation.
- The Mintz invitation must be completed within **10 days** or a new submission will be required.
- Fingerprinting and a phone interview are **conditional steps** and may not be required for every employee.
- The Declaration Kit requires a witness who is **over 18 and is not the employee**.
- The employee headshot must have a **white background**.
- Allow up to **48 hours after completion of the process** for the security profile and site access to be finalized.

## Contact / Assistance

For assistance with the Bank of Canada security screening process, contact:

**facilities-RFA@bank-banque-canada.ca**

This contact should also be used if the Mintz invitation has not been received within 48 hours after submitting the PIR.
