## Purpose

This document defines the process for communicating and managing employee absences and vacations within the Candiac branch.

The objective is to ensure absences are properly requested, communicated, and visible to the team so that staffing and daily operations can be planned accordingly.

---

# Planned Absence Procedure

For a planned absence or vacation, follow the steps below.

## Step 1 - Submit the Request

The employee must submit the absence request through:

```text
OnePay

https://portal.people.adp.com/oneux/ui/#/dashboard

```

---

## Step 2 - Request Confirmation

The appropriate manager will:

- Review and confirm the request.
- Add the approved absence to the shared **Candiac Branch** calendar.

Once added to the shared calendar, the absence becomes visible to other employees using that calendar.

---

## Step 3 - Notify the Team

On the day before the planned absence, the employee must send an email to the team advising that they will not be present.

The notification should be sent to:

- Technicians
- Office personnel

This ensures that everyone affected by the absence is aware before the employee leaves.

---

# Planned Absence Workflow

```text
Employee Plans Absence
        ↓
Submit Request in OnePay
        ↓
Manager Confirms Request
        ↓
Absence Added to Candiac Branch Calendar
        ↓
Day Before Absence
        ↓
Employee Emails Team
        ↓
Absence
```

---

# Last-Minute Absence

If an employee is sick or encounters an unexpected issue that prevents them from reporting to work:

Contact the appropriate manager directly and advise them of the absence.

The manager will then notify the rest of the team by email.

This process applies to situations where the normal planned absence procedure cannot reasonably be followed.

---

# Candiac Branch Shared Calendar

The shared **Candiac Branch** calendar is used to provide visibility into employee vacations and absences.

Employees can enable this calendar in Outlook to see planned absences across the branch.

---

## Displaying the Shared Calendar

Within Outlook:

1. Open the **Calendar**.
2. Locate the shared or group calendars.
3. Enable:

```text
Candiac Branch
```

Once selected, the Candiac Branch calendar will appear alongside the employee's personal calendar.

---

# Viewing Calendars Separately

The employee's personal calendar and the **Candiac Branch** calendar can be displayed side by side.

Example:

```text
Personal Calendar       Candiac Branch
-----------------       --------------
Meetings                Vacations
Appointments            Absences
Personal Schedule       Branch Availability
```

This view makes it easier to compare personal commitments with branch staffing.

---

# Combining the Calendars

Outlook also allows the personal calendar and Candiac Branch calendar to be displayed together in an overlay view.

To combine the calendars:

1. Display both calendars.
2. Locate the arrow beside the **Candiac Branch** calendar heading.
3. Select the arrow to overlay the shared calendar with the personal calendar.

This allows meetings, appointments, vacations, and absences to be viewed together on a single calendar.

The calendars can also be separated again when desired.

# Holiday Vacation Planning

Vacation requests for the holiday period must be decided by:

```text
End of October
```

When planning holiday vacations, staffing requirements must be considered.

At least:

```text
One person from each department
```

must remain available.

Employees should therefore coordinate holiday vacation requests early enough to maintain minimum departmental coverage.

A reminder will normally be communicated before the holiday vacation planning deadline.

---

# Employee Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for:

- Submitting planned absence requests through OnePay.
- Waiting for confirmation of the request.
- Sending an absence notification email to the team the day before a planned absence.
- Informing the appropriate manager directly when an unexpected absence occurs.
- Reviewing the Candiac Branch shared calendar when planning vacations or absences.
- Planning holiday vacations before the end-of-October deadline.

---

# Manager Responsibilities

The appropriate manager is responsible for:

- Reviewing absence requests.
- Confirming approved requests.
- Adding approved absences to the Candiac Branch shared calendar.
- Communicating unexpected or last-minute absences to the team when required.
- Maintaining visibility of branch staffing through the shared calendar.

---

# Common Mistakes

## Request Submitted but Team Not Notified

Submitting an absence through OnePay does not replace the employee's responsibility to notify the team.

For a planned absence, send the team notification email the day before leaving.

---

## Absence Not Visible on Shared Calendar

Approved planned absences should be added to the **Candiac Branch** shared calendar so the team can account for employee availability.

---

## Last-Minute Absence Not Communicated

For illness or another unexpected absence, contact the appropriate manager as soon as possible so the team can be notified.

---

## Holiday Vacation Requested Too Late

Holiday vacation plans should be established by the end of October.

Requests must also account for the requirement to maintain at least one available employee from each department.

---

# Guiding Principle

Absence management depends on clear and timely communication.

Planned absences should be requested, approved, added to the shared calendar, and communicated to the team in advance.

Unexpected absences should be communicated to the appropriate manager as soon as possible so that branch operations can be adjusted accordingly.
