# Clover - Invoice to Pay

# Last Updated: 2026-09-02

## Purpose

This procedure explains how to use **Clover Invoice to Pay** to send a customer an email payment link.

The customer uses the link to enter their own payment information directly in Clover, avoiding the need to provide credit card information over the phone or by email.

## Objectif

Cette procédure explique comment utiliser **Clover Invoice to Pay** afin d'envoyer au client un lien de paiement par courriel.

Le client peut ainsi saisir lui-même ses informations de paiement directement dans Clover, sans avoir à transmettre ses informations de carte de crédit par téléphone ou par courriel.

# Accessing Invoice to Pay

Log into Clover.

From the landing page, select either:

- **Virtual Terminal**
- **Invoices**

Both options can be used to access the invoice creation process.

---

# Creating an Invoice

## Via Virtual Terminal

Access the invoice functionality through the **Virtual Terminal**.

## Via Invoices

1. Select **Invoice** from the Transaction Type drop-down.
2. Select **Create New Invoice**.

Either method will take you to the Clover **Invoice** page.

---

# Entering Invoice Information

On the Invoice page:

1. Enter the payment amount.
2. Enter the applicable **invoice or order number** in the **Note** field.

---

# Selecting the Customer

Add a new customer or locate an existing customer.

Once the customer has been selected, the **Create Invoice** button becomes available.

Select the appropriate delivery options when required.

Then select:

```text
Create Invoice
```

---

# Customer Payment

After the invoice has been created, the customer receives an email from Clover.

The customer selects the:

```text
Pay
```

link contained in the email.

This opens the Clover payment page.

The customer then enters their own payment information and completes the transaction directly through Clover.

---

# Monitoring the Invoice

To review existing invoices:

1. Select **Invoices** from the left-hand menu.
2. Locate the applicable invoice.

The Invoices page displays the current status of the invoice.

Additional invoice functions are available through the **three-dot menu** located on the right side of the invoice.
