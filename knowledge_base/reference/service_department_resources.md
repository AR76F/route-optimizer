## Purpose

This document provides quick access to the primary software applications, websites, and SharePoint resources used by the Service Department.

These resources are used daily by Service Coordinators for work order management, technician scheduling, reporting, payment processing, warranty lookup, and route planning.

---

# Core Software

## Business Management System (BMS)

**Purpose**

Primary ERP system used to manage:

- Customers
- Work Orders
- Quotations
- Units
- Sites
- Billing
- Invoicing

**Access**

https://onebmscan.cummins.com/forms/frmservlet?config=12c_canada_eng

---

## FieldAware

**Purpose**

Field service management platform used for:

- Technician scheduling
- Dispatching
- Appointment management
- Field reports

**Access**

https://app.fieldaware.com/account/jobs_and_quotes/scheduler

---

## Power BI

**Purpose**

Reporting platform used for:

- Work In Progress (WIP)
- Performance reporting
- Operational dashboards
- KPI monitoring

**Access**

https://app.powerbi.com/groups/me/apps/ed3dd510-050a-47ed-9455-477e60121d5d/reports/f18265a3-a16f-4eaa-ac0e-1ff1630c4117/ReportSection37e3466f482fcfc46c49?experience=power-bi

## Clover

**Purpose**

Payment processing platform used for:

- Credit card pre-authorizations
- Customer payments
- Refunds
- Customer payment profiles

**Access**

https://www.clover.com/login
