# Shared Mailbox - Service Department

# Last Updated: 2026-09-01

## Purpose

This procedure defines how the **Service Department shared mailbox** is monitored, triaged, assigned, and processed.

The objective is to ensure that every email has an assigned owner, urgent requests are handled immediately, and no email is deleted.

## Objectif

Cette procédure définit le processus de gestion, de triage, d'assignation et de traitement de la **boîte courriel commune du département de service**.

Elle couvre notamment le triage des courriels, les niveaux d'urgence, la répartition par secteur, les délais de réponse et le traitement des demandes clients.

# 1. Shared Mailbox Principle

The shared mailbox is the single entry point for customer requests.

Every email must have a responsible person at all times.

**Never delete an email.**

---

# 2. Sector Assignment

Route emails according to the customer's sector.

Current sector assignments are maintained in the **Service Department Directory**.

If the assigned person is absent, the designated replacement takes over.

---

# 3. Triage

The designated person performs mailbox triage:

- In the morning at opening.
- At the beginning of the afternoon.

Apply the appropriate urgency color:

| Color | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Red | Urgent |
| Orange | Service |
| Blue | Quotation / Billing |
| Green | Treated / Completed |

Preventive Maintenance requests do not belong to the Service Department.

Redirect PM requests to the **PM Department** and file the email accordingly.

Move each email into the appropriate sector folder:

- South Shore / North Shore
- Montréal
- Ottawa

For **urgent (Red)** emails, immediately notify the responsible person for the sector verbally.

---

# 4. Sector Processing

The responsible person should review their sector folder:

- In the morning.
- At the beginning of the afternoon.
- With priority given to Red / urgent emails.

The responsible person must:

1. Send an acknowledgement to the customer.
2. Process the request through to the final response.
3. Mark the email **Green** once treated.
4. Move the completed email to the **Archives** subfolder of the appropriate sector.

Emails received between scheduled triage periods may be classified or taken by the first responsible person who sees them.

---

# 5. Response Times

| Request Type | Acknowledgement | Target Response |
|---|---:|---:|
| Urgent | 30 minutes | Immediate handling |
| Service | 2 hours | 1 business day |
| Quotation / Billing | 4 hours | 3 business days |

---

# 6. Internal Emails

Cummins employees sending an email to the shared mailbox must include the **sector and urgency** in the subject.

Use:

```text
[SECTOR] - [URGENCY] - Description - BT #
```

Example:

```text
MTL - URGENT - Generator Failure - BT 123456
```

## Sectors

```text
RS-RN = South Shore / North Shore
MTL = Montréal
OTT = Ottawa
```

## Urgency

```text
URGENT = Unit out of service
SERVICE = Plannable service request
ADMIN = Quotation / Billing
```

Send **one request per email**.

Do not combine multiple customers or unrelated subjects in one email.

Include the essential information in the first email:

- Customer
- Site address
- Unit number
- Work Order number, if available

For urgent requests, also contact the responsible person by phone or direct message.

An email alone is **not considered an adequate urgent notification**.

---

# 7. Rules

- Always reply from the **shared mailbox**, never from a personal mailbox.
- Keep the original email subject.
- Add the OneBMS Work Order number to the subject when applicable.
- Move emails sent to the wrong sector immediately to the correct folder and notify the responsible person.
- Never delete an email.
- Keep the shared mailbox empty at the end of the day, except for active requests.
