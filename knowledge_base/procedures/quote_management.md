# Procedure - Preparing and Accepting a Quote

# Last Updated: 2026-09-03

---

## Purpose

This procedure explains how Service Coordinators prepare and process customer quotations following a Request for Quote (RFQ) submitted by a technician.

The technician's RFQ provides the technical information and parts requirements identified during a service call or other work. The Service Coordinator uses this information to prepare the actual quotation (WOQT) in BMS, coordinate parts pricing, send the quotation to the customer, and process the quotation once customer approval is received.

## Objectif

Cette procédure explique comment les coordonnateurs de service préparent et traitent les soumissions destinées aux clients à la suite d'une demande de soumission (RFQ) remplie par un technicien.

Le RFQ du technicien fournit les renseignements techniques et les pièces requises identifiés lors d'un appel de service ou d'autres travaux. Le coordonnateur de service utilise ces renseignements pour préparer la soumission (WOQT) dans BMS, coordonner le prix des pièces, envoyer la soumission au client et la traiter une fois l'approbation du client reçue.

# RFQ and Customer Quotation

The **Request for Quote (RFQ)** and the **customer quotation (WOQT)** are two different parts of the quotation process.

### Request for Quote (RFQ)

The RFQ is completed by the **technician** when additional work, repairs, or parts are identified during a service call or other planned work.

The RFQ provides the technical information required by the Service Coordinator to prepare the quotation.

### Customer Quotation (WOQT)

The actual customer quotation is prepared by the **Service Coordinator** in BMS as a **WOQT**.

The coordinator uses the technician's RFQ to determine the required work, create the WOQT, coordinate parts pricing with the Parts Department, and prepare the quotation that will ultimately be sent to the customer.

---

# Procedure

## 1. Retrieve RFQ from FieldAware

Following a service call or other planned work, the technician may submit an RFQ when additional work or parts are required.

Using the FieldAware Job ID provided by the technician:

1. Open the job in **FieldAware**.
2. Scroll to the **TASKS** section.
3. Locate **Request for Quote (RFQ)**.
4. Open the RFQ.
5. Download or print the RFQ, or capture the required information through a screen capture.

The RFQ should contain the information required to understand the additional work and parts identified by the technician.

---

## 2. Create the WOQT in BMS

Create a **WOQT** in BMS using the information provided in the technician's RFQ.

Prepare the WOQT with the required labor, travel, and other applicable charges.

Parts can then be coordinated with the Parts Department.

---

## 3. Send Parts Request

Send the parts request to the Parts Department.

Include:

- WOQT number
- RFQ file
- Parts list
- Urgency of the WOQT

When providing a printed RFQ or other physical document, write the **WOQT number** and your **name** on the document.

## 4. Complete the Customer Quotation

Once the required parts information and pricing have been added, complete the WOQT and prepare the quotation for the customer.

The quotation should clearly describe the proposed work and applicable charges based on the technician's findings.

---

## 5. Send the Quotation to the Customer

Send the completed quotation to the customer.

When sending the email:

- Include the quotation number in the email subject.
- Attach the quotation.
- Inform the customer that approval is required before proceeding.
- Request a Purchase Order (PO) and/or signed quotation when applicable.

---

# Customer Approval

A quote is approved when:

- Signed quote is returned

OR

- Customer explicitly approves by email

Retain the customer's approval as part of the quotation/work order documentation.

---

# After Customer Approval

Once the quotation has been approved, continue with the appropriate workflow based on the customer's payment type.

- **Charge Customers** – Convert the WOQT into a Work Order and continue with the standard scheduling process.
- **Cash Customers** – Follow the **Cash Customers - Accepted Quote** procedure before scheduling work.

---

# Quote Accept

When **"Quote Accept"** is specified:

- Parts Department assumes customer approval.
- WOQT may be converted directly into WO.

> **Important**
>
> Only use **Quote Accept** when customer approval has been confirmed.
