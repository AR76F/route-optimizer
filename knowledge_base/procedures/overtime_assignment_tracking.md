# Procedure - Overtime Assignment and Tracking 

# Last Updated: 2026-09-03

## Purpose

This procedure defines how evening and weekend overtime opportunities are recorded and assigned within the Candiac Service Department.

The objective is to maintain a fair overtime allocation process by considering both:

- Technicians who have performed overtime.
- Technicians who volunteered for overtime but were not selected.

Accurate and timely maintenance of the overtime tracking file is required to provide a fair view of each technician's participation.

## Objectif

Cette procédure définit comment les occasions de temps supplémentaire en soirée et durant les fins de semaine doivent être enregistrées et attribuées au sein du département de service de Candiac.

L'objectif est d'assurer une répartition équitable du temps supplémentaire en tenant compte des techniciens qui ont effectué du temps supplémentaire ainsi que de ceux qui se sont portés volontaires sans être sélectionnés.

# Scope

This procedure applies to:

- Evening jobs.
- Weekend jobs.
- Overtime opportunities communicated through an open call to technicians.

Every applicable overtime job must be recorded in the overtime tracking file.

# Recording Interested Technicians

When an open call for overtime is made, record **every technician who volunteers**, regardless of whether they are ultimately selected.

For each technician who provides their name:

Enter:

```text
I
```

The `I` indicates that the technician expressed interest in the overtime opportunity.

This information is important because overtime participation is evaluated based not only on overtime actually performed, but also on opportunities for which technicians volunteered without being selected.

---

# Recording the Selected Technician

Once the technician has been selected for the overtime job:

Add a:

```text
Green X
```

for the selected technician in the overtime tracking file.

The green `X` identifies the technician who was ultimately assigned to and performed the overtime job.

---

# Technician Selection

Overtime assignments are determined using a combination of:

1. Previous overtime performed.
2. Seniority.

The objective is to distribute overtime opportunities fairly among technicians.

---

## Previous Overtime

Before assigning the job, review the technician's recent overtime history.

A technician who recently performed overtime may be passed over in favor of another interested technician who has not recently received an overtime opportunity.

---

## Seniority

Seniority is considered when selecting among technicians.

However, seniority does **not automatically mean that the most senior technician receives every overtime assignment**.

Recent overtime history must also be considered.

---

# Selection Example

Louis and François both volunteer for an overtime job.

Assume:

- Louis has greater seniority.
- Louis performed overtime during the previous weekend.
- François did not perform overtime during the previous weekend.

In this situation:

```text
François is prioritized for the current overtime assignment.
```

Even though Louis has greater seniority, François receives priority because Louis recently received an overtime opportunity.

The objective is to maintain equitable overtime distribution over time.

---

# Why Interested Technicians Must Be Recorded

Recording only the technician who ultimately performs the job does not provide a complete picture of overtime participation.

For example:

```text
Technician A
Volunteered 5 times
Selected 1 time

Technician B
Volunteered 1 time
Selected 1 time
```

If only completed overtime is recorded, both technicians appear to have participated equally.

Recording expressions of interest provides visibility into technicians who consistently volunteer even when they are not selected.

---

# Coordinator Responsibilities

For every evening or weekend overtime opportunity:

- Record the overtime job in the tracking file.
- Record every technician who volunteers.
- Mark each interested technician with an `I`.
- Review previous overtime assignments.
- Consider technician seniority.
- Select the technician according to the overtime allocation process.
- Mark the selected technician with a green `X`.
- Ensure the file is updated after the job.

---

# Common Mistakes

- **Recording only the selected technician:** Every technician who volunteered must be recorded with an `I`, even if they were not selected.
- **Selecting based only on seniority:** Seniority is one factor, but recent overtime performed must also be considered.
- **Missing evening or weekend jobs:** Every applicable overtime job must be entered into the tracking file.
- **Delaying file updates:** Update the overtime tracking file after every applicable job so the information is current for the next assignment.

---

# Guiding Principle

The overtime process should provide every technician with a fair opportunity to participate.

Accurate tracking of both **interest** and **actual overtime performed** is essential to maintaining an equitable overtime allocation process.
