# Cash Customers - Overview

# Last Updated: 2026-09-02

## Policy Overview
Cash customers create higher financial risk because no approved credit account exists.

## Objectives

The objectives of the Cash Customer procedure are to:

- Reduce financial risk.
- Ensure payment is collected promptly.
- Minimize expired pre-authorizations.
- Improve cash flow.
- Reduce Days Sales Outstanding (DSO).

### Key Principles

#### No Guaranteed Payment

Service work without payment protection exposes the branch to financial risk.

#### Financial Risks

Examples include:

- Expired pre-authorizations.
- Customer disputes.
- Delayed invoicing.
- Forgotten follow-ups.

#### Pre-Authorization Is Not Payment

A pre-authorization:

- Does not guarantee collection
- Must be converted to final payment

#### Cash Flow Protection

Prompt collection protects:

- Cash flow
- Financial stability
- Operational efficiency

### Coordinator Expectations

- Obtain payment before dispatch whenever required.
- Follow up with the supervisor after service completion.
- Contact the customer within the same week.
- Convert pre-authorizations promptly.
- Produce and send the invoice within the same week.
- Follow up immediately
- Convert pre-authorizations quickly
- Close completed jobs promptly
