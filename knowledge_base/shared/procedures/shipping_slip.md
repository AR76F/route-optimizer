# Shipping Slip

## Purpose

This procedure explains how to complete and submit a shipping slip when equipment or parts must be shipped to a customer, another branch, a technician, or a warranty destination.

## Objectif

Cette procédure explique comment remplir et remettre un billet d'expédition lorsqu'un équipement ou une pièce doit être expédié à un client, une autre succursale, un technicien ou un destinataire de garantie.

---

## When to Use a Shipping Slip

Use a shipping slip when:

- Sending a load bank to a customer site or another branch.
- Sending parts to a technician or another branch.
- Sending a part to a warranty destination.
- A carrier is scheduled to pick up the shipment.

---

## Required Information

Complete the following sections on the shipping slip.

### Ship To / Expédié à

Enter:

- Customer site, branch, or other delivery destination
- Name of the contact who will receive the shipment
- Contact's telephone number

### Our Reference / Notre référence

Enter the applicable **Work Order number**.

### Quantity and Description / Quantité et description

Enter:

- Quantity being shipped
- Clear description of the equipment or parts
- Relevant equipment or part identification, when available

### Note

Add any useful shipping, delivery, or handling instructions.

---

## Submit the Shipping Slip

1. Put on the required personal protective equipment.
2. Bring the completed shipping slip to Mario, Jean-Carl or Justin in the Shipping Department.
3. If all of them are unavailable, leave the document on their desks.
4. Write your name on the shipping slip when leaving it on the desk.

---

## Important Notes

- Complete all required sections before submitting the document.
- Confirm the delivery destination and contact information.
- Include the Work Order number as the internal reference.
- Use a clear description so the Shipping Department can identify the shipment.
