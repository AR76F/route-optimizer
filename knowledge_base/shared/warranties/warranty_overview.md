# Warranty Overview

## Purpose

This document provides a general overview of the warranty processes handled by Service Coordinators.

Warranty claims are technical processes that depend on the equipment, failed component, warranty coverage, technician diagnosis, and information available in Cummins warranty systems.

Use this document to identify the applicable warranty path and the information required before beginning a claim.

For detailed claim creation and processing steps, refer to the applicable warranty procedure.

---

## Main Warranty Categories

The warranty process can be divided into three main categories:

### 1. Generator Engine Warranty — CECO

Use the engine warranty process when the failed component belongs to the engine.

For generator applications:

- CECO components are associated with the engine warranty.
- Engine warranty information and failure codes are verified through QuickServe Online (QSOL).
- The generator serial number can be used in PGBU to locate the corresponding engine serial number when required.

The exact warranty coverage must be verified for the affected engine and component before proceeding with the claim.

---

### 2. Generator Warranty — Onan

Use the Onan warranty process when the failed component belongs to the generator rather than the engine.

For generator applications:

- Onan components are associated with the generator warranty.
- Warranty coverage is verified through PGBU.
- Generator failure codes can be found through PGBU under:

  `Tools & Resources → Warranty Coverage Lookup → Fail Code Inclusion List`

If the generator's Date in Service does not populate in PGBU, the unit may not be registered and registration may be required before proceeding.

---

### 3. Electric Bus Warranty — CECO

Electric bus warranty claims use the CECO warranty process and share several concepts with engine warranty claims.

Before determining warranty coverage, obtain and verify the required information for the bus and failed component.

Electric bus warranty claims rely heavily on QuickServe Online and the Warranty Failure Code Manual for Electrification to identify the applicable component and failure code.

The warranty documentation must also be reviewed for applicable limitations and exclusions.

---

## Information Required Before Evaluating Warranty

Before proceeding with a warranty claim, collect as much of the following information as applicable:

- Unit serial number / Engine Serial Number (ESN)
- Date in Service / Warranty Start Date
- Current operating hours or mileage
- Failed component
- Root cause of the failure
- Progressive damage, if applicable
- Technician diagnosis and report
- Work order number
- Location of the repair: in-shop or field service
- Applicable warranty coverage

Do not assume that the manufacturing or build date is the Warranty Start Date. Verify the applicable Date in Service.

---

## Technician Diagnosis

The technician's diagnosis and report are critical to the warranty process.

The technician must identify the failed component and document the cause of the failure. The report is then used to determine information such as:

- Failure Code
- Root cause
- Progressive damage
- Repair actions performed
- Applicable Standard Repair Times (SRTs)
- Time requiring additional justification

Do not modify the technician's report to make it fit the warranty claim. If information is missing or requires correction, ask the technician to update the report.

---

## Failure Codes

Failure Codes depend on the equipment and failed component.

### Generator — Onan

Use PGBU to locate the applicable generator Failure Code.

### Generator Engine — CECO

Use QuickServe Online and the Warranty Failure Code Manual to locate the applicable engine Failure Code.

### Electric Bus — CECO

Use the QuickServe Online Warranty Failure Code Manual for Electrification.

The correct Failure Code must correspond to the component and failure identified by the technician.

When the correct code is unclear, confirm the failure with the technician rather than selecting a code based only on assumptions.

---

## Warranty Coverage

Warranty eligibility must be verified for the specific unit and failure.

Depending on the equipment, coverage can be checked using:

- QuickServe Online
- PGBU
- Guidanz
- Cummins warranty documentation and manuals

For electric buses, the training documentation also identifies the Cummins Warranty AI Assistant as an available warranty resource.

Always review applicable warranty limitations and exclusions when determining coverage.

---

## Standard Repair Times (SRT)

Standard Repair Times represent the allowed time associated with specific repair operations.

SRT selection must be based on the actions documented in the technician's report.

Depending on the product:

- Generator SRTs can be searched through PGBU.
- Engine SRTs can be searched through QuickServe Online.
- Electric bus SRTs are selected based on the repair actions performed and available SRT information.

If the required repair operation cannot be found, additional warranty procedures may be required, including documented use of applicable non-standard labor codes.

Refer to the dedicated SRT and labor warranty procedure for detailed instructions.

---

## Important Principles

- Always verify warranty coverage for the specific unit and failed component.
- Do not assume that a component is covered solely because the unit is under warranty.
- Base the claim on the technician's documented diagnosis and repair actions.
- Confirm unclear Failure Codes with the technician.
- Verify the Date in Service / Warranty Start Date.
- Document progressive damage when additional components were damaged as a consequence of the root cause.
- Warranty claim information and narratives must accurately reflect the work that was actually performed.
- Ensure warranty-covered labor and parts are properly allocated before closing the work order.
- Warranty invoices must be verified before finalization.

---

## When the Procedure Is Unclear

Warranty claims can contain product-specific requirements, exceptions, coverage limitations, and situations that are not fully documented in this knowledge base.

If the applicable procedure or coverage cannot be confidently determined:

1. Do not assume the missing warranty requirement.
2. Verify the applicable warranty information in QuickServe, PGBU, Guidanz, or the relevant Cummins warranty documentation.
3. Confirm technical information with the technician when necessary.
4. Escalate the question to an experienced warranty resource when the available documentation does not provide a clear answer.

The Service Assistant should be used as a reference to support the warranty process, not as a replacement for warranty-system verification or technical judgment.
