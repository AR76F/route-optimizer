# Distributor Warranty Overview

## Purpose

Distributor warranty is a separate warranty path used when the repair is covered through the distributor warranty arrangement. It must be distinguished from the standard manufacturer or product warranty path before the claim is created.

The source material documents an Onan distributor-warranty workflow in BMS. Other distributor-warranty products, vendors, coverage rules, or Claim Types require their own validated procedures.

## Information to Confirm

Before creating a distributor warranty claim, confirm:

- Customer and Work Order information
- Unit or generator information
- Technician diagnosis and repair details
- Failed component and root cause
- Warranty coverage source
- Whether the work was performed in-shop or in the field
- Covered percentages for parts and labor
- Applicable vendor and Claim Type

Do not assume that all parts, labor, travel, or towing on a Work Order are covered. Verify each applicable charge against the distributor warranty terms.

## Relationship to Other Warranty Paths

A generator component may follow an Onan warranty path, an engine or CECO warranty path, or a distributor warranty path. The failed component and coverage source determine which procedure applies.

If the same Work Order contains more than one warranty path or claim, follow the applicable multi-claim and charge-allocation procedure before finalizing the invoice.

## Documented Procedure

For the BMS steps documented in the distributor presentation, see [Distributor Warranty Claims](distributor_warranty_claims.md).

## Source Limitations

The presentations do not provide a complete distributor warranty policy, coverage table, exclusion list, reimbursement policy, or approval matrix. Confirm those items in the current official warranty documentation before applying coverage.

## Résumé en français

La garantie du distributeur constitue une voie distincte de la garantie standard du fabricant ou du produit. Les documents disponibles décrivent un processus de garantie du distributeur Onan dans BMS. Avant de créer la réclamation, confirmer la source de couverture, la pièce défectueuse, la cause, le lieu du travail, le fournisseur, le type de réclamation et les pourcentages applicables aux pièces et à la main-d’œuvre.
