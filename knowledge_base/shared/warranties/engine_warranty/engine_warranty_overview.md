# Generator Engine Warranty Overview

## Purpose

This document describes the CECO engine-warranty path for a generator when the failed component belongs to the engine rather than the generator assembly. Use it together with the shared warranty procedures.

## When This Path Applies

Use the engine warranty path when:

- The failed component is an engine or CECO component.
- The technician's diagnosis identifies an engine-related failure.
- The applicable coverage verification confirms engine warranty coverage.

If the failed component belongs to the Onan generator assembly, follow the generator or Onan warranty procedure instead. If the repair is covered through a distributor warranty arrangement, follow the distributor warranty procedure.

## Required Information

Before creating the claim, confirm:

- Engine serial number or ESN
- Generator serial number when needed to identify the engine
- Date in Service or Warranty Start Date
- Failure date
- Current operating hours
- Failed component and root cause
- Technician diagnosis and repair actions
- Repair location: in-shop or field service
- Applicable CECO coverage

Do not assume that a generator's coverage automatically covers every engine component. Verify the unit and component in the applicable warranty system.

## Coverage and Technical References

Use QuickServe Online (QSOL) to:

- Review the unit's warranty information.
- Confirm the applicable coverage.
- Access the **Warranty Failure Code Manual**.
- Search for applicable Standard Repair Times.

Guidanz, PGBU, or other Cummins warranty resources may also be required when the engine or Date in Service information cannot be confirmed through the primary source.
